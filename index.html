Our Sacred Vision

Repurposing Temple Offerings with Love

At Organic Fragrances, we believe in restoring the harmony of nature. Every year, millions of tons of flowers are offered in sacred temples across India, only to be discarded in our pristine rivers later. We steps in to give these sacred blossoms a beautiful second life.

We meticulously collect these fresh floral offerings, sun-dry their petals, and blend them with natural essential oils, resins, and organic herbs. Handrolled by local women artisans, our dhoop sticks are 100% charcoal-free, preserving pure floral essence and uplifting communities.
